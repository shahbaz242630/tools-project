# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-….md) | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Relates to:** BRD §X.Y

## Context

What situation forced a choice? Include the constraint that actually mattered — a provider limitation, a legal requirement, a failure mode we hit. Keep it factual; save the argument for the decision.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What follows, including the parts we do not like. An ADR listing only benefits is marketing, not a record.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. This is usually the most valuable section a year later — it stops the same rejected option being proposed again, and it shows whether the decision was actually reasoned.

## What would change this

The condition under which we should revisit. A decision with no stated trigger tends to outlive its context.
